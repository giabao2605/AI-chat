# Chat workspace

Mode: Operate. Code-led. Target: public/index.html and its runtime panels.

## Direction contract

THESIS: A personal conversation space for watching several AI participants, with a readable transcript and controls that remain easy to find.

OWN-WORLD: User-pinned ChatGPT reference: neutral charcoal canvas, darker left sidebar, system sans-serif, quiet dividers, white primary action and text labels instead of decorative symbols.

STORY: Choose a topic or let AI choose, start, watch and join. History, statistics and inspection stay available without dominating the conversation.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Desktop left sidebar around 280px, fluid main chat, compact session toolbar, centered readable transcript and bottom composer. Empty state offers the existing start actions. Signature interaction: collapse the sidebar to give conversation the full workspace, reopen with a clearly named control. Motion limited to subtle hover/focus and respects reduced motion.

FORM: Familiar chat workspace, explicitly pinned by the user; no random concept selection. seed: none (user-pinned direction).

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
